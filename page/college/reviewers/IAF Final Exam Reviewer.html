IAF Final Exam: Quick Review

Read, then start the self-test. Questions and choices reshuffle on every refresh.

Framework and equation

  • Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Equity = Assets − Liabilities. If equity is 25% of assets, liabilities are 75%.
  • Economic entity: business is separate from its owner. Going concern: business continues. Time period: life divided into periods. Accrual: record when earned/incurred, not when cash moves.
  • Accountancy Act of 2004 (RA 9298) professionalizes and regulates accountancy. IFRS 18 (presentation and disclosure) is effective Jan 1, 2027.

Analyzing transactions and trial balance

  • Debit = left. Debit increases assets, expenses, drawings. Credit increases liabilities, equity, revenue.
  • Trial balance proves debits = credits only. It will NOT catch omitted transactions, wrong accounts, or equal offsetting errors. Transposition error: difference divisible by 9.

Adjusting entries and worksheet

  • Deferrals: prepaid expense (Dr Expense, Cr Asset), unearned revenue (Dr Unearned Revenue, Cr Revenue). Accruals: accrued expense (Dr Expense, Cr Payable), accrued revenue (Dr Receivable, Cr Revenue).
  • Depreciation = (Cost − Salvage) ÷ Life. Unadjusted TB → Adjustments → Adjusted TB → Income Statement → Balance Sheet columns.
  • Skipping an expense adjustment overstates assets and profit.

Statements, closing, reversing

  • Order: Income Statement → Changes in Equity → Balance Sheet. Ending capital = beginning + investments + profit − drawings.
  • Close revenues and expenses to Income Summary, Income Summary to Capital, then Drawing to Capital. Post-closing TB has permanent accounts only.
  • Reversing entries (optional) simplify later cash payments/receipts for accruals.

Merchandising

  • Periodic: COGS = Beg Inv + Net Purchases + Freight-in − End Inv. Net purchases = Purchases − Returns − Discounts. Perpetual: Dr COGS, Cr Inventory at each sale.
  • FOB shipping point: buyer pays freight, title passes at shipping point. FOB destination: seller pays, title passes at destination.
  • Trade discount reduces list price (not recorded). Cash discount (2/10, n/30) rewards prompt payment. Markup on cost: price = cost × (1 + %). Gross profit rate = GP ÷ Net sales. VAT 12%: VAT-inclusive price ÷ 1.12.

Special journals

  • Sales journal: credit sales. Purchases journal: credit purchases. Cash receipts: all cash in, including cash sales. Cash disbursements: cash out. General journal: everything else.
  • Subsidiary ledger total must equal the control account.

Manufacturing

  • Costs: direct materials, direct labor, overhead. Selling and admin expenses are NOT manufacturing costs. Flow: Raw Materials → WIP → Finished Goods → COGS.
  • DM used = Beg RM + Purchases − End RM. Prime = DM + DL. Conversion = DL + OH. COGM = Beg WIP + Total mfg cost − End WIP. COGS = Beg FG + COGM − End FG.
  • Predetermined OH rate = Estimated OH ÷ Estimated base.

Payroll

  • Gross pay is before deductions. Net pay = Gross − SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax and other deductions.
  • 13th month pay = 1/12 of total basic salary earned in the calendar year. De minimis benefits are small, tax-exempt. Use an imprest payroll account; separate timekeeping from payroll preparation.

Partnership, corporation, sustainability

  • Without agreement, partners share profit and loss equally. Salary and interest are allocated first, the remainder by ratio. Bonus after bonus: B = rate ÷ (1 + rate) × profit.
  • Corporation: Revised Corporation Code, no minimum capital stock. Issue above par → Share Premium. Treasury shares reduce equity. Dividend declaration debits Retained Earnings; paying it reduces cash.
  • SDGs: 17 goals by 2030. SASB: industry-specific material issues.

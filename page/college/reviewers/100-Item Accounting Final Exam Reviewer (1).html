Core Accounting Principles Summary

Review key concepts, formulas, debit/credit rules, and adjusting entries prior to taking the 100-item self-assessment test.

Basic Accounting Equation
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
  • Assets: Resources owned with economic value (Cash, AR, Inventory, PPE).
  • Liabilities: Debts/Obligations owed to third parties (AP, NP, Unearned Rev).
  • Equity: Residual interest of owners (Capital - Drawings + Net Income).
Rules of Debit & Credit
Assets, Expenses, Drawings Dr (+) / Cr (-)
Liabilities, Equity, Revenue Dr (-) / Cr (+)
Contra Assets (Accum. Dep) Normal Balance: Credit
Adjusting Entries
  • Accrued Expense: Dr Expense / Cr Liability
  • Accrued Revenue: Dr Asset (AR) / Cr Revenue
  • Prepaid Expense: Dr Expense / Cr Asset
  • Unearned Revenue: Dr Unearned Rev / Cr Revenue
  • Depreciation: (Cost - Salvage) / Useful Life
Inventory Costing
  • FIFO: First-In, First-Out. Ending inventory reflects latest prices. COGS is lower in inflation.
  • Weighted Average: Total Cost of Goods Available ÷ Total Units Available.
  • Periodic vs Perpetual: Periodic computes COGS at end of period using physical count; Perpetual updates inventory per transaction.
Bank Reconciliation
Adjusted Book Balance:
+ Interest Earned / Notes Coll.
- Bank Service Charges / NSF Check
Adjusted Bank Balance:
+ Deposit in Transit
- Outstanding Checks
Essential Ratios
  • Current Ratio: Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities
  • Quick Ratio: (Cash + Quick Sec. + AR) ÷ Current Liabilities
  • Gross Profit Rate: Gross Profit ÷ Net Sales
  • Profit Margin: Net Income ÷ Net Sales

How to use the NU GWA Grade Calculator

The NU GWA Calculator is a free tool for students of National University. Estimate your General Weighted Average based on your course grades and unit loads in seconds in different computations.

For straight computation

  1. Enter the course name, numeric grade point (from 0.0 to 4.0), and units.
  2. Add more courses using the + Add course button at the bottom of the table.
  3. It will automatically calculate your GWA instantly for midterm or final.
  4. Delete course list by clicking the delete button.

For term computation

  1. Enter the course name, numeric grade point for midterm and final, and units
  2. Add more courses using the + Add course button at the bottom of the table.
  3. It will automatically calculate your final semester course GWA instantly.
  4. Delete course list by clicking the delete button.

For per course breakdown computation

  1. Enter the course name, units, and switch label whether if its for midterm or final.
  2. Enter the name of category.
  3. Enter the percentage equivalent for each category.
  4. Enter the total score and your score of each task.
  5. It will automatically calculate your final semester course GWA instantly.
  6. Add more categories by clickingg + Add Category button before the exam category for additional category that set by a course or professor.
  7. Add more courses using the + Add Course button at the bottom of the table for separate breakdown.
  8. Delete course list by clicking the delete button.
NOTE: Midterm and final wasn't computed in combine. Try add separarte list for same course if you want to compute both midterm and final then use the term computation calculator to calculate the grades of the entire term.

How GWA is Computed

GWA is calculated by multiplying each course grade by its unit value, summing all results, then dividing by total units included in the calculation.

For straight computation

GWA =
(Grade1 × Units1) + (Grade2 × Units2) + ... + (Graden × Unitsn)
(Units1 + Units2 + ... + Unitsn)

Example: 4.0 in 3 units + 3.5 in 2 units + 3.0 in 6 units → (12.0 + 7.0 + 18) ÷ 11 = GWA 3.36

For term computation

Course Term Grade = (Midterm Grade × Midterm Weight in %) + (Final Grade × Final Weight in %)

NOTE: The term grade per course is not officially a final term grade by the institution. We can use the values to compute the GWA for the term.
Term GWA =
(Term Grade1 × Units1) + (Term Grade2 × Units2) + ... + (Term Graden × Unitsn)
(Units1 + Units2 + ... + Unitsn)

Example:
Course Midterm Final Units Term Grade
40% 60%
Accounting 2.5 3.0 6.0 2.8
Contemporary World 3.5 4.0 3.0 3.8
IT Era 4.0 4.0 3.0 4
12.0 Units GWA 3.35

For course breakdown computation

Category1 =
(Score1 + Score2 + ... + Scoren)
(Total Score1 + Total Score2 + ... Total Scoren)
= Initial %tage over 100% × Weight in %

Categoryn =
(Score1 + Score2 + ... + Scoren)
(Total Score1 + Total Score2 + ... Total Scoren)
= Initial %tage over 100% × Weight in %

Exam = Score ÷ Total Score = Initial %tage over 100% × Weight in %

Course Grade = Weighted Category1 + Weighted Category2 + Weighted Categoryn + Weighted Exam

NOTE: After computing the final percentage, refer to the table of the official grading system to convert into point grade.

Qualifications

  • Attain a Term General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 3.25
  • Carry a minimum academic load of 12.0 units unless specified otherwise in the program flowchart. Note: if the regular load for the term falls below 12.0 units, have the Registrar's Office validate your qualifying GWA for the term on or before the midterm period of the following term.
  • No final grade below 2.5 in any course
  • No failing grade of F, R, or 0.0 in any course.
  • No Officially Dropped (Dr) in any course.
  • No incomplete (Inc) grade in any course at the time of award.
  • Not have been found guilty of cheating or academic dishonesty as provided for in the NU's Handbook.
  • No record of disciplinary action for committing a major offense as determined by the Student Discipline Office.

Tips to Improve Your GWA


Courses with more units have a greater impact on your overall GWA. A 6-unit course affects your GWA far more than a 3-unit course. So focus your energy where it counts most.

A grade of 0.0 lowers your average and does not meet this calculator's honors thresholds. If you're struggling, seek help early rather than letting it reach that point.

Use this calculator after each term to check your GWA. Course grades and exam scores are different: this tool can calculate by list of courses and the course's breakdown into individual activities.

Retaking a failed subject and passing it improves your transcript and academic standing. Treat remedial courses as a second chance, not an afterthought. Note that some major courses have limits on retakes; excessive failures and retakes may result in disqualification from the program.

Taking too many units when you're not prepared can hurt your performance across all subjects. Plan your term wisely and know your limits. You can request load revision from your registrar two weeks from the opening of classes every term.

I offered the free ride for Pasay City West High School students who rode the LRT-1 from Libertad going to EDSA or Baclaran station. The offer is implemented to help students to save their spending from riding the LRT-1 train that is intended to save and use it for school activities and personal spendings due to fare hike to all railway lines. Originally, the free ride program runs from April 1 to November 28, 2023.

However, as the song Light Rail Transit Line 1 went viral, I offer it again starting March 5, 2024.

Students can meet with me to avail and enjoy the free train rides that students can feel the cold air conditioning, fast speeds, beautiful outside view and convenient.

The program was stopped during the transition of new school year 2024–2025 in order to prioritize academic and financial needs.

Background

In 2022, when the news appeared that the fare hike in LRT systems are placed in 1st quarter of 2023. I decided to make an special offer to Pasay City West High School students, the free train rides in LRT-1 that who rode from Libertad to EDSA and Baclaran stations.

As the hike is not yet to be implement, the program officially starts from April 1 until April 30, 2023. On April 27, 2023, two students were given a train ride for free by availing the free ride. After two students availed the free LRT-1 rides, the program was extended its duration until May 15 then extended again to June 30.

On July 3, only few students are attending the Pasay City West High School so I marked the free ride program ended on June 30.

On August 2, 2023, the fare hike was implemented. The fare from Libertad to Baclaran/EDSA is still ₱15, only stored value card does (from ₱12/13 to ₱15). As most students rode with purchased signle journey ticket, the hike was no effect for short rides.

On August 23, 2023, I announced that the program will be implemented again on August 29 until November 28, 2023 due to fare hike was implemented and to help students to save for personal and school purposes. And on November 28, 2023, the program ends.

After few months of inactivity to this program, on March 4, 2024, I announced that the program will be took in place on March 5 to honor his best friend's birthday. After the song Light Rail Transit Line 1 went viral, the program is extended starting in the following day until someone has availed during the school year 2023–2024.

Unfortunately, only two students availed the free train rides, officially the end of free ride program.

How to avail the free ride

  • Step 1 - you have to meet with me at the given time and place provided.
  • Step 2 - write your complete name, grade and section and station destination in my logbook.
  • Step 3 - wait to let me to pay and process your ticket without spending your own.
  • Step 4 - claim your ticket and enjoy your free ride.

There's no limit how many free rides you want as long as you're a student of Pasay City West High School and going to Baclaran or EDSA station from Libertad.

Reminder

Free rides program was offered from me. Do not tell tellers and staffs of LRT-1 for free rides because this offer can get from me.

FAQ

Question Answer
Who can avail the free ride? Free ride is only available for students of Pasay City West High School.
Where is only available? It is only available at Libertad station southbound only (going to Baclaran or EDSA stations only).
Is free ride mandatory? No, we cannot do if they are not interested with free rides program.
I don't meet him at given place and time, what should I do? Just wait or possibly have an emergency. If not, try to contact me or have a meet in the school.
When and how long the free rides last? From April 1, 2023 to May 16, 2024
Is this program affiliated with LRTA or LRMC? No, this program is not affiliated with Light Rail Transit Authority or Light Rail Manila Corporation and they don't offer free rides in LRT-1 since the line is privatized.
